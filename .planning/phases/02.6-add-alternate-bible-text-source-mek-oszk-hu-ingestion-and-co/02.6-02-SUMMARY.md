## Decisions Made
- Whitespace comparison collapses all whitespace (spaces, tabs, and newlines) using `" ".join(text.split())` after NFC-composing the string, preventing false mismatch flags on formatting variants.
- The detailed discrepancy view in stdout is limited to the first 100 items to avoid flooding the terminal or degrading system performance.
